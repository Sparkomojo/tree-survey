# Route Notes

Daily site notes for highway tree surveys, on your phone: the route, From and To (what3words and grid reference), distance,
team, species, defects, constraints and access, extra notes and photos with captions – saved as a PDF you can share.

**Open it:** https://sparkomojo.github.io/tree-survey/route-notes/ – then add it to your home screen (Chrome: ⋮ → *Add to Home screen*;
iPhone: Share → *Add to Home Screen*). It works without signal once it has been opened once.

- **📍 Here** fills the grid reference from GPS. With your own what3words API key (☰ → Settings) it fills the three words too.
- **Save PDF** makes `Site_Notes_DD_MM_YYYY_route.pdf`: the note, then the photos four to a page.
- **New day** keeps the route, team and species; yesterday's To becomes today's From.
- Everything stays on your phone. Nothing is uploaded. Save the PDF before clearing your browser's data.

Route Notes is the route notes page of the Tree Survey app, on its own.
